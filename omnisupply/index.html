Fleet exposure and planning at your fingertips.

A hurricane is five days out from Texas. You have twenty aircraft and an afternoon to decide where they go. Ask the question the way you would say it out loud — and get the answer, with the evidence behind it, before the meeting ends.

  • 01
    Ask like a person. No query language, no analyst queue. Type the question you actually have and the answer comes back in seconds, in the same English you asked it in.
  • 02
    Every number brings its receipt. Source, as-of date and confidence sit on the face of each figure. A number cannot render without them — that rule lives in the schema, not the style guide.
  • 03
    Run the week before you live it. Pick a placement strategy and watch a hundred simulated weeks play out. The strategies come back ranked, so you commit aircraft to the one that won.
  • 04
    It keeps working after you close it. Exposure reviews, competitive analysis and trip retrospectives arrive every month on their own — a new look at how you acted and where to improve.

79 seconds. Sound on. Figures are read from public flight, freight, border-crossing and storm records. The placement simulator is a synthetic sandbox and says so on screen.
