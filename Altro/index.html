Altro

A travel ranking app for logging cities, comparing past trips, and building a personal map of the places that still pull you forward. Altro turns travel memories into a clear, ranked view of taste and return energy.

View App
Travel Rankings Personal Map Mobile

What it does

Your cities, ranked the way you actually feel about them

Log Every City

Add each city you visit with a quick review and a personal rating. Your travel history builds itself as you go.

Rank and Compare

See all your cities in a single ranked list. Compare trips side by side and discover which places actually pulled you back.

Personal Map

Build a living map of everywhere you have been, colored by how strongly each place calls you back.

Return Energy

Not just where you went, but how much each place still pulls you. Altro captures the feeling, not just the fact.
