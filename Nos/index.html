Common questions
What is Nos?
Nos is a prayer and journaling app for novenas, daily reflection, and honest journaling. It gives structure without pressure and beauty without clutter.
What is a novena?
A novena is a nine-day prayer devoted to a specific intention. Nos provides a curated library of novenas organized by intention and tracks your progress day by day.
Does Nos use streaks or pressure notifications?
No. Nos is built around the belief that prayer is a relationship, not a performance metric. There are no streaks, no guilt, and no notifications that shame you for missing a day.
Is Nos available on iOS and Android?
Nos is designed for both iOS and Android mobile devices.
