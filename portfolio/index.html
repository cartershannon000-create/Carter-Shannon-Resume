Common Questions
What is CS Ventures' approach to building products?

CS Ventures builds products that start with a clear problem and end with a working solution. Each product is designed to solve a specific need for real users, built with a focus on clarity, functionality, and a direct path from idea to delivery. Every product CS Ventures ships addresses something that was genuinely missing, never a hypothetical use case.

What is DeckForge and who is it for?

DeckForge is an autonomous AI agent that writes equity research decks, critiques its own output, and rewrites until the composite quality score clears a defined bar. It is designed for analysts, investors, and advisors who need board-ready research output without the manual iteration cycles that typically consume hours of analyst time.

What is Remit and is it a real product?

Remit is a fully working mock of an accounts-receivable agent, built to show the anatomy of an autonomous AI system rather than to sell one. It demonstrates the full loop: a scheduled run with no human prompt, a tool-calling reasoning engine, an append-only decision record for audit, a human-gated morning digest, and a weekly refinement pass that writes versioned rules to memory. The ledger data is simulated, but every component and interaction on the page mirrors how the production pattern works.

What is Altro and what problem does it solve?

Altro is a travel ranking and mapping app that solves the problem of travel memory being scattered and unstructured. Most people cannot tell you which cities they have visited in ranked order, or which ones they actually want to return to. Altro gives you a single ranked list of every city you have visited, a personal map colored by return energy, and the ability to compare trips side by side.

What is Nos and what makes it different from other prayer apps?

Nos is a prayer and journaling app centered on novenas and daily reflection. Unlike most prayer apps, Nos is built without streaks, guilt mechanics, or notifications designed to shame you for missing a day. It treats prayer as a relationship rather than a performance metric, providing structure without pressure and a clean, distraction-free experience.
