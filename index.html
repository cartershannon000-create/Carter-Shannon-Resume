How we work

A short path from question to working result

1

Scope

A short discovery to define the question, the users, and what done looks like. You get a written scope before any build starts, so there are no surprises about what you are paying for.

2

Build

Work ships in tight loops. You see working software and real analysis early and often, not a single reveal at the end. Feedback lands while it is still cheap to act on.

3

Deliver

Delivery includes documentation and a clean handoff. The result is yours to run, and CS Ventures stays available for the questions that come up after launch.

Three ways to engage: advisory work when you need analysis and a recommendation, fixed-scope builds when you need a product or tool shipped, and ongoing partnerships when the work is continuous.

About CS Ventures

What is CS Ventures?

CS Ventures is an independent product and strategy studio founded by Carter Shannon. It enables individuals and small businesses to create new products, optimize business processes, gain more traction in their markets, and identify new opportunities. CS Ventures operates at the intersection of strategic consulting, data analytics, and hands-on product development.

Who founded CS Ventures?

CS Ventures was founded by Carter Shannon, a strategy and analytics professional based in Washington, D.C. Carter works at a Big 4 consulting firm within its Government and Public Sector practice and brings experience across financial risk, government technology, and go-to-market strategy to everything CS Ventures builds.

What products has CS Ventures built?

CS Ventures has shipped work in three categories. In agents and AI: DeckForge, an autonomous agent that writes and self-refines equity research decks until they are board-ready, and Product Team Loop, an autonomous product team of agents that finds, ranks, and ships its own backlog. In apps and websites: Altro, Nos, and the Altro travel marketing site. In trainings and papers: a first-app development guide, an AI-to-EBITDA strategy deck, and an SMB process-automation deck.

How can I contact CS Ventures?

You can reach CS Ventures by email at info@cs-ventures.us or by phone at (336) 455-3421.
