Common Questions

About CS Ventures

What is CS Ventures?

CS Ventures is an independent product and strategy studio founded by Carter Shannon. It enables individuals and small businesses to create new products, optimize business processes, gain more traction in their markets, and identify new opportunities. CS Ventures operates at the intersection of strategic consulting, data analytics, and hands-on product development.

Who founded CS Ventures?

CS Ventures was founded by Carter Shannon, a strategy and analytics professional based in Washington, D.C. Carter works at Deloitte Consulting within the Government and Public Sector practice and brings experience across financial risk, government technology, and go-to-market strategy to everything CS Ventures builds.

What products has CS Ventures built?

CS Ventures has built three products: DeckForge, an autonomous AI agent that writes and self-refines equity research decks until they are board-ready; Altro, a travel ranking app for logging cities and mapping return energy; and Nos, a prayer and journaling app for novenas and daily reflection.

How can I contact CS Ventures?

You can reach CS Ventures by email at info@cs-ventures.us or by phone at (336) 455-3421.
