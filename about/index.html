About

Carter Shannon

Technology has automated the deterministic layer of business. Data pipelines run without analysts. Reports generate in real time. Decisions that once required teams now require a prompt. The bottleneck has shifted from execution to judgment.

What that creates is a new kind of demand: for people who can think clearly about what technology makes possible, decide what is actually worth building, and move an idea from concept to delivery without losing the thread. That gap between idea and execution is where most organizations stall. Filling it requires strategic thinking, systems fluency, and the discipline to ship.

Carter Shannon works at that intersection. As a consultant at Deloitte's Government and Public Sector practice, he builds analytics systems, guides technology adoption, and delivers financial and operational outcomes for federal and state clients. As the founder of CS Ventures, he designs and ships products from scratch. Both require the same skill: knowing which problem is worth solving, building the system that solves it, and driving it to delivery.

His path spans financial diligence and demand modeling at Logistics Capital and Strategy, public sector technology and analytics at Deloitte, go-to-market strategy for Anthropic's AI models in the federal market, and product development across AI agents, mobile apps, and analytical systems. The thread across all of it is the ability to translate what is technically possible into decisions and tools that actually move organizations forward.
